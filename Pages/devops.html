What is DevOps?

Unifying Development and Operations

DevOps is a set of practices that combines software development (Dev) and IT operations (Ops) to shorten the development lifecycle and provide continuous delivery of high-quality software. As a DevOps engineer, you'll build and maintain the infrastructure and tools that enable teams to develop, test, and release software rapidly and reliably.

Cloud engineering, often closely related to DevOps, focuses on designing, implementing, and managing applications and infrastructure in cloud environments like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, enabling scalability, reliability, and cost efficiency.
