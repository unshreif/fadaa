Track Overview

UI/UX Design is the process of creating digital products that are both aesthetically pleasing (UI) and functionally intuitive (UX). This track takes you from the fundamentals of design thinking to creating polished, interactive prototypes ready for development.

You'll learn user research methods, information architecture, wireframing, prototyping, visual design principles, and usability testing. By the end of this track, you'll have a professional portfolio showcasing your ability to design beautiful, user-centered digital experiences that solve real problems.
